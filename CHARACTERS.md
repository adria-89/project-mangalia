# Mangaliene. - Characters

Personality sheet for the regulars. Edit freely; the in-game data lives in
`customer/<name>.tres` (lines, tastes, voice, jiggle). When you change a
personality here, update her lines/tastes there to match (and vice versa).

All characters are adults.

## Lizard - the excitable dock goofball

**File:** `customer/lizard.tres`

**Personality**
- Loud and clingy. Opens with "Ahoy!", talks in exclamation marks.
- Loses fights to crabs, keeps a fish she found in a boot, mistook a seagull
  in a little shirt for Messi.
- Flirts openly, then panics and backs off ("...Forget I said that").
- Owes the boss money, plans to pay him back in shells, hopes he never comes back.

**Voice / sample lines**
- "Ahoy again! I'm back! Did you miss me? Say yes."
- "You're cuter than Messi though. Don't tell Messi."
- Happy: "OHHH. That's the stuff. You're hired. By me."
- Angry: "Did you pour it with your feet?"

**Tastes**
- Favorite: Beer
- Sometimes: Motorină
- Drunk orders: Prenadez, Mangaliene
- Hates: Cola ("Cola?? Do I look like a CHILD to you?")

**Numbers**
- Tip (good / meh): 4 / 1
- Voice pitch: 1.25
- Bust: medium, jiggle stiffness 110 (the softest of the three regulars)

## Cat - the deadpan tsundere

**File:** `customer/cat.tres`

**Personality**
- Cold and curt ("Don't talk. Just pour."). Insists she doesn't like you,
  plainly comes back to see you.
- Uses mundane errands as excuses to spend time with you: "Pig help. Not a date."
- Running gags: the walrus who owes the whole port money, La Lidia selling
  her a pig instead of fish, the neighbor singing off-key sea shanties.
- Dry and dramatic when you get it wrong.

**Voice / sample lines**
- "You again." / "...Not that I came here to see you."
- "Don't smile at me. I can hear you smiling."
- Meh: "I've had worse. At funerals."
- Angry: "This is an insult to my ancestors."

**Tastes**
- Favorite: Beer
- Sometimes: Rum, Cuba Libre
- Hates: Motorină ("Motorină. Do I look like a tugboat to you.")

**Numbers**
- Tip (good / meh): 2 / 1
- Voice pitch: 0.8
- Bust: medium, jiggle stiffness 190 (firm)

## Strigoi "Striggy" - the would-be scary demon who's a sweetheart

**File:** `customer/strigoi.tres` (the dev's OC, the least lewd of the cast)

**Personality**
- Tries hard to be menacing ("Feared across all of Dobrogea. Um."), then
  apologizes for being too loud.
- Shy, stammery, relentlessly polite: even a botched drink gets "B-but thank you!"
- Tried to haunt the lighthouse; the keeper made her tea. Hissed at a
  seagull; they're friends now. Secretly likes the nickname Striggy.
- First time ever in a bar.

**Voice / sample lines**
- "M-mortal! I have RETURNED!" / "...Was that too loud? That was too loud. Sorry."
- Happy: "Mmh! D-delicious... like the blood of my enemies!"
- Angry: "I will curse you. A small curse. A t-tiny one."

**Tastes**
- Favorite: Cola ("the closest thing to blood you've got")
- Sometimes: Cuba Libre ("feeling brave": rum is fine hidden in cola)
- Hates: Rum ("R-RUM?! It smells like a church!"): burns like holy water
- Won't try: Prenadez, Mangaliene (rum floating on top, not hidden)

**Numbers**
- Tip (good / meh): 3 / 1
- Voice pitch: 1.1
- Bust: small, jiggle stiffness 230 (the stiffest)
